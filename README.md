## 📱 About Me

> **Always On Display is love.
> Local LLMs are the future.
>  Old devices deserve a second chance.**

Hey there! I’m a dev who loves messing with Android under the hood, building custom ROMs, writing systemless mods, and self-hosting my own private ecosystem.

I’m not just about installing tools and calling it a day—I love digging into how the Android framework operates, tweaking system resources, hunting down weird compatibility bugs, compiling custom recoveries, and squeezing every drop of performance out of older tech.

What keeps me busy:

* 🔧 Crafting custom ROMs, kernels, custom recoveries, and device trees.
* 🧩 Writing Magisk modules, Runtime Resource Overlays (RRO), and systemless tweaks.
* ⚙️ Writing Shell scripts, automating repetitive tasks, and administering Linux boxes.
* 🤖 Self-hosting local LLMs via Ollama (mostly Qwen 3.5 and Gemma 3 1B Uncensored).
* 🏠 Running my home server, private networking, and self-hosted apps.
* 🔌 Tinkering with microcontrollers like ESP32 and Arduino for hardware monitoring.
* 🚀 Setting up GitHub Actions CI/CD to build heavy projects without melting my local machines.

I have a soft spot for low-spec hardware—especially older 32-bit ARM Android devices with minimal RAM that everyone else wrote off.

Sometimes I break things. Usually, I fix them up even better.

**What I'm focused on right now:** Android framework customization, recovery/ROM builds, RRO experiments, lightweight AI workflows, and polishing my home server setup.

### 📦 MagiskMicroGApps

A super clean, systemless setup for Google services alternatives on Android.

It strips out bloated Google binaries and replaces them with a lightweight setup using MicroG (GmsCore, GsfProxy, FakeGApps), signature spoofing support, and Aurora Store.

* ⚡ Keeps background RAM usage under 50MB—perfect for potato phones.
* 🔋 Huge boost to battery life and system responsiveness.
* 🧩 100% systemless Magisk module implementation.

[![GitHub Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/elmendezz/MagiskMicroGApps?style=flat-square&color=00AF91&logo=github)](https://github.com/elmendezz/MagiskMicroGApps)  
Repository: [MagiskMicroGApps](https://github.com/elmendezz/MagiskMicroGApps)

## 🏠 Self-Hosted Infrastructure

I prefer hosting my own services so my data stays mine, away from third-party cloud lock-in.

### 📸 Immich — Photo & Video Backup
> *Managing around 200,000 photos and videos entirely on my own hardware.*

Immich is the heart of my server: a fast Google Photos alternative that backs up my massive family photo library without relying on cloud subscriptions.

### 🛡️ AdGuard Home
DNS-level adblocker running across my entire home network to filter ads, malware, and telemetry before they hit any device.

### 🔗 Tailscale
My private mesh VPN connecting servers, desktops, and mobile phones into one secure network without opening public ports.

### 🌐 Nginx Reverse Proxy
Handles SSL certificates, custom domain routing, and internal web services like my student admin app (Alumnia).

### 🐳 Docker & Debian Linux
The foundation of my home server environment, running modular containerized services managed over SSH.
